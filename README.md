# UNZA Ridgeway Self-Medication Research Dashboard

A private research dashboard and findings summary for Lusale Esther’s cross-sectional study of self-medication among healthcare students at the University of Zambia, Ridgeway Campus.

## Files

- `UNZA_Self_Medication_Research_Dashboard.xlsx` — Excel dashboard with seven worksheets, 13 editable charts, summary tables, an interactive school filter, analysis notes and source/analysis-ready response tabs.
- `Research_Findings_and_Methods.md` — findings, methods, data-quality caveats, association tests, exploratory adjusted analysis and proposal review.
- `Dashboard_Preview.pdf` — one-page printable preview of the Excel overview.

## Headline result

Of 383 respondents, 344 reported self-medication in the previous 6–12 months (89.8%, Wilson 95% CI 86.4%–92.5%). Among those respondents, 49.1% reported antibiotic use. Refer to the findings report for denominators and limitations.

## Important interpretation and privacy notes

This is a cross-sectional, voluntary-survey sample; the descriptive prevalence should not be interpreted as a weighted campus estimate, and the data do not establish causality. Several respondents who answered “No” also supplied follow-up behaviour answers; those answers are flagged and excluded from behaviour summaries.

**This repository is private. The Excel workbook includes participant-level, coded survey responses in its Raw Data and Cleaned Data worksheets. Do not change repository visibility or share the workbook outside the authorized research team without reviewing the study’s consent, ethics and data-sharing requirements.**
